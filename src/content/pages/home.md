I'm a master's student in theoretical computer science at Université de Montréal,
advised by Dmitry Sokolov and Pierre McKenzie. I work on proof complexity and
communication complexity, and I'm especially interested in where these lower-bound
techniques meet practice.

Outside of theory, I build low-latency systems in C++. I also write here about
algorithms, complexity, and engineering. See the [blog](/posts/).
